4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Some Ozzy data is stored locally using device storage, including onboarding state, app preferences, active timer state, local game progress, notification scheduling records, and Screen Time shield configuration. Local data may remain on your device until you delete it, delete your account, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

Apple's Family Controls framework uses privacy-preserving tokens for selected apps, categories, and web domains. Ozzy may store those tokens locally or in the app group required for the shield extension, but we do not send the specific selections to our backend.

5. Photo Verification and AI Processing

When you choose to verify a task with a photo, Ozzy sends the task name and image to our backend, which submits them to Google Gemini API for image analysis. The AI returns whether the task appears completed, a confidence score, and a short explanation.

We do not intentionally store task photos after the verification request completes. We store usage counters and verification metadata such as scan counts and timestamps to enforce feature limits, prevent abuse, and operate the service. Service providers may process or transiently retain inputs and outputs as described in their own terms and policies.

Ozzy uses Google's Gemini API, currently Gemini 2.5 Flash, on paid quota through a billing-enabled Google Cloud project to analyze task photos and task descriptions. When Ozzy uses paid Gemini API services, Google states that it does not use prompts, associated files such as images, or generated responses to improve Google products, and processes prompts and responses under Google's applicable Data Processing Addendum. Google may log prompts and responses for a limited period solely to detect and prevent violations of its policies, maintain the safety and security of the services, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations. Ozzy does not intentionally store task photos after verification completes; we store verification metadata and scan counters as described in this Policy.
